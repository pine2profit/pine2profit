### A1 What does the script compute and which signals does it emit?
The script computes three EMAs of the close: 8, 21 and 55 bars (lines 8–10). The "bull" state is 8 > 21 > 55, "bear" is 55 > 21 > 8 (lines 11–12). The three lines are plotted (lines 13–15) and the background is tinted by state (line 16). Two `alertcondition` calls fire on the bar where a state has just formed (lines 17–18). The author intends the bull flip as a long entry and the bear flip as an exit or a short entry.

| output | line | type | role intended by the author |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast / Mid / Slow | 13–15 | line | visualisation |
| bgcolor bull/bear | 16 | colour | regime |
| Ribbon turns bullish | 17 | event | long entry |
| Ribbon turns bearish | 18 | event | exit / short entry |

### A2 Which signal is known at bar close and can be placed in an alert?
Both `alertcondition` calls depend only on close-based EMAs and on the previous bar's value, so they are final at bar close and go into an "Once Per Bar Close" alert without rewording. Tradeable signal: "Ribbon turns bullish" for entry, "Ribbon turns bearish" for exit. Example signal bar: [[t:2025-11-19T18:00:00Z@117678]] — close 117,678; on the next bar the entry [[trade:65]] fills at 117,678.
