### A1 What does the strategy do?
Long entry when SMA(20) crosses above SMA(50) (lines 11–12), position closed when it crosses below (lines 13–14). No shorts, stops, targets or filters. `strategy()` settings (line 4): capital 10,000, position size 100% of equity, commission not set (0), slippage not set, default execution — next bar's open, no pyramiding.

| element | line | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| entry | 11–12 | crossover(SMA20, SMA50) → long |
| exit | 13–14 | crossunder(SMA20, SMA50) → close |
| size | 4 | 100% of equity, compounding |
| commission / slippage | 4 | not set = 0 |

### A2 Can the strategy's signals be executed through alerts?
Yes: crossings are evaluated at bar close and orders fill at the next bar's open — that is exactly the alert regime. Example: signal bar [[t:2025-11-19T04:00:00Z@115884]], fill [[trade:89]] at 115,884 on the next bar. Neither `process_orders_on_close` nor `calc_on_every_tick` is on; there is no stop or target, so same-bar ordering does not arise.
